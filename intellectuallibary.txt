# 🖥️ MISSION: Develop "Intellectual OS" (v1.1)

**CONTEXT:** High-end web OS for stealth productivity & gaming.  
**STYLE:** Ultra-Modern Glassmorphism, Deep Space Navy (#000105), Glowing Intel Blue Accents.  
**VIBE:** Professional, hand-crafted developer aesthetic.

---

### 🟢 [PHASE 1: THE HYPER-PRECISION BOOTLOADER]
* **The Global Cursor:** Replace default system cursor with a custom **Fixed Crosshair (+)**. 
    * **Design:** 1px thick lines, Intellectual Blue (#004cff). 
    * **Behavior:** On hover over interactive targets (buttons/ASCII), the crosshair lines must expand from 40px to 60px and glow white/blue.
* **The Layout:** Dual-column ASCII Art centered at the top of the viewport.
    * **Left Column:** Arch Linux Logo (Color: #ffffff) with a slow 30s Y-axis rotation animation.
    * **Right Column:** "INTELLECTUAL" Text Art (Color: #00a2ff) with a permanent glowing text-shadow.
* **The Terminal:** Rapid-fire scrolling "Intellectual Kernel" logs below the art.
    * **Logs:** Must include `LINKING_EXTERNAL_PROTOCOLS...` and `SUCCESS: discord.gg/intelligent`.
* **The Rain:** Once logs finish, initiate a **Deep Blue Digital Rain** (#003366) overlay.
    * **Opacity:** Keep it at 0.4 so the background remains atmospheric but readable.
* **The Network Node:** A clickable link `discord.gg/intelligent` at the bottom. It must glow on hover and open in a new tab.
* **Transition:** On click of the [ ENTER ] button, trigger a "Digital Cut"—a 2s pitch-black fade before snapping to the desktop.

---

### 🔵 [PHASE 2: LOGIN & STEALTH SETTINGS]
* **Login UI:** 30px rounded glassmorphism cards with `backdrop-filter: blur(25px)`.
* **Stealth Suite:** * **Cloaking Mode:** Toggle to change Browser Tab Title to "Google Classroom" and Favicon to a Google Drive icon.
    * **Themes:** 20-swatch accent color picker to adjust the system glow (Primary: #004cff).
    * **Persistence:** Export/Restore JSON config for localStorage.

---

### 🌐 [PHASE 3: THE INTELLECTUAL BROWSER]
* **UI Layout:** Custom window with centered URL bar, "Shield/Proxy" status icon, and glowing navigation arrows.
* **Function:** High-performance `<iframe>` loader with deep-blue glass aesthetic.

---

### 🎮 [PHASE 4: INFINITY GAME ENGINE & ERROR HANDLING]
* **Launcher:** Black background loading screen with a rounded blue progress bar.
* **Robustness:** Display custom error: `CONNECTION REFUSED - ROUTING VIA BACKUP...` if frames fail.
* **Resource Management:** Completely destroy `<iframe>` elements on window close to free up RAM.

---

### 💎 [PHASE 5: WINDOW PHYSICS & POLISH]
* **Visuals:** 28px border-radius, 1px glowing borders, and high-intensity backdrop blurring.
* **Interactions:** Draggable headers, resizable edges, and "Ghost Preview" snapping for full-screen.
* **Performance:** Maintain 60FPS using `requestAnimationFrame` for all UI movement.
